Welcome to CrossPad

CrossPad is a portable instrument for making music: a sampler, a sequencer, a recorder and a mixer in one device, with sixteen backlit pads that respond to pressure, a touch screen and an encoder. It runs on a battery, has speakers and microphones built into the case, and connects to a computer with a single USB cable, through which it is at the same time a MIDI controller and a sound card.

With CrossPad you can:

  • play kits of samples on the pads: drum kits, loops, cut-up breaks, melodic instruments;
  • record rhythms in the sequencer and arrange them into whole songs;
  • record sound from the microphones, the line inputs, the pads or the computer, cut it into slices and make your own kits out of them;
  • mix the inputs and outputs, and route sound to the headphones, the line out and the computer;
  • drive a music program on the computer (FL Studio, Ableton Live, REAPER).

How to read this manual

  • Words from the screen are written as they appear on the CrossPad, in a box: LOAD, EDIT, SWING.
  • The pads are numbered from 1 to 16: pad 1 is in the lower left corner, pad 16 in the upper right.
  • A click of the encoder is a short press of the knob, a hold is a press of about half a second.
  • "Back" is a short press of the power button, described in the Handling chapter.
This manual describes firmware v1.1.4 CrossPad gets updates: you install new versions in the Firmware app (the Updates chapter). The newest documentation is at crosspad.app/docs.

Safety

  • Charge CrossPad from a USB charger or from a computer. Do not leave it for long in the sun or in a car.
  • Set a low volume before you put headphones on. Listening to loud music for a long time damages hearing.
  • The microphones and the speakers are in one case: sending the microphones to the speakers, or holding headphones against the CrossPad, can cause feedback (a squeal). Turn that output down, or switch that send off in the Mixer.
  • Do not take the microSD card out while CrossPad is writing (recording, saving a kit, exporting).
