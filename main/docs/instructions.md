# CrossPad

Get started

## Charge

- Charge from the main USB port on the top edge. The second USB port does not charge it.
- The battery icon at the top right shows the level. It turns red when the battery runs low.
- A bolt beside it means the battery is taking charge. Plugging a charger in shows the level and the charge current for a few seconds; Settings > Info shows them too.
- A nearly empty battery switches the CrossPad off. It does not switch on again until it is charged: the status light blinks three times instead.

## Power button

- Switch on: hold 1.5 s, then let go. Nothing lights up while you hold.
- Switch off: hold 2.4 s, then let go. Change it in Settings > System > Power Long-Press.
- Ship mode: on battery, a 4.2 s hold cuts the battery for storage. To wake it, plug a charger into the main USB port or hold the button for about 1 s.
- Short press: back one step. It closes a list or a message, ends a value change, then closes the app. On the home screen it does nothing.
- Hold about 0.5 s, then let go: jump to the transport bar at the top of the screen. Do it again to jump back.

## MODE map

The home screen is the MODE map: one cell per pad, laid out like the pads. Pad 1 is bottom-left, pad 16 top-right. Hit a pad, tap a cell, or turn the encoder to it and press.

- 1 Sampler: play a kit on the pads
- 2 Seq: Sequencer, record loops
- 3 Song: chain scenes into a song
- 4 Arrange: eight tracks
- 5 Record: Recorder, mics, line in, pads or USB
- 6 Slicer: cut a recording into a kit
- 7: empty
- 8 More: the other apps
- 9 Pitch: Pitch Play, where your firmware has it
- 10 Pad Mix: one fader per pad
- 11 Mixer: inputs, outputs and routing
- 12 DAW: DAW Control for your music software
- 13 Kits: the kit browser
- 14 Help: this page
- 15 Update: install firmware updates
- 16 Setup: Settings

In an app, tap MODE (four small squares, top left) to bring the map back. Tap MODE again to stay in the app.

## Move around

- Encoder: turn to move, press to choose. On a value, press, turn, press again.
- Touch: tap to choose, drag to scroll, swipe right to go back.

## Kits

- Kits live on the microSD card. Keep the card in its slot.
- No kit is loaded at start-up: the Sampler (pad 1) first opens the kit browser.
- Kits (pad 13) or a tap on the kit name in the Sampler opens the browser.
- The tabs on the left sort what is on the card.
- PERF: performance kits, long and short samples. + NEW KIT makes an empty one.
- DRUMS: drum kits, short hits. + NEW DRUMS makes an empty one.
- SLICE: one recording cut into a pad each.
- PITCH: one sound across the pads, and instruments saved in Pitch Play.
- SONGS: your songs. Loading one opens Arrange.
- USER: your Recorder takes, which open in the Slicer, and kits you made before.
- Turn to a kit and press, or tap it, to load it.

## Volume

- Settings > Audio > Output Volume: the Speakers and Headphones sliders, 0 to 100.
- Speakers (auto on play) off keeps the built-in speakers silent.
- The Mixer (pad 11), OUT tab: one fader per output.

## USB

The main USB port works in one of two modes. Choose it in Settings > USB > Mode.

- MIDI + Audio (default): USB MIDI and a sound card, Crosspad Audio.
- MIDI + Serial: USB MIDI and the link for browser updates.

The second USB port is a MIDI port of its own: CrossPad MIDI+Serial.

## Power saving

- Without use, the screen dims after 10 minutes and the CrossPad switches off after 30 minutes.
- Change both in Settings > System: Dim After and Sleep After.
